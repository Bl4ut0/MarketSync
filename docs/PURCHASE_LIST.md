# Purchase a crafting list

On the classic **Search Auctions** page, select a craft-linked list in the
Shopping breakout and scan the list. Hover **Purchase List** for its live quoted
total, quantities to buy, and any whole-stack extras. Incomplete or stale scans
cannot authorize buying the list.

Click **Purchase List** to review the stack-by-stack plan. The planner chooses
the lowest actual unit price first. It does not substitute a higher-priced
small stack just to avoid buying extra units in a cheaper large stack. Extras
and any later, higher price tiers are disclosed explicitly.

Use **Check next stack**, then **Buy Stack** to confirm its displayed price and
quantity. Each purchase needs a user click. Exact stack verification happens
immediately before buying; missing stacks and changed prices stop the queue.
Refresh list prices and reopen Purchase List to approve a replacement plan.
Changing craft requirements also invalidates the plan. There is no automatic
purchase from a timer or silent substitution of a more expensive stack.

The queue advances only after the server confirms the purchase and the receipt
matches the selected craft's remaining requirement. Errors, closing the Auction
House, and confirmation timeouts stop buying. After a timeout, check receipts
and rescan rather than assuming the purchase failed or retrying blindly.

The craft receives the units it actually needs and their proportional actual
cost. The item ledger records the entire purchased stack and total paid, so
extra units retain their acquisition cost for later use. Quantities and prices
are rechecked for every stack; hover totals remain estimates until confirmed.

This queue currently targets the classic Search Auctions page shown in the
request. Modern commodity purchases continue through their existing controls.
